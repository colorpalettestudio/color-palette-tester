---
name: Contrast terminology
description: User's preference for how contrast testing is presented
---

Use numeric contrast scores and let users change the testing benchmark. Do not reference WCAG in the tool's user-facing language.

**Why:** The user asked to present contrast scores instead of WCAG naming.

**How to apply:** Use a single selector: All pairings, 3:1, 4:1, 5:1, or 7:1. A numeric option shows pairings at or above that score. Do not restore the old separate benchmark and meets/below filter rows or their summary counts.

Keep Step 2 focused on comparing and choosing; the Download button belongs in Step 3.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested these choices, removed the meet/below counts, and asked to remove the extra Step 2 Download button.

**How to apply:** Preserve chosen pairings across ratio changes, and keep their actual numeric contrast scores visible independently of the active filter.
