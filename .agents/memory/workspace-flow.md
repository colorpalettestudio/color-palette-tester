---
name: Workspace flow
description: User-approved three-step structure and scope of the interface simplification
---

Organize the tool as three visible sections on one page: Add your colors, Compare & choose, and Download your palette. Do not make users complete separate wizard screens or click Next/Back.

**Why:** The user approved the first-pass interface improvements and a three-step structure to make the existing tool more intuitive.

**How to apply:** Keep the palette editor near the top, use live contrast updates, and place comparison results before the chosen-pairing collection on mobile. Keep detailed educational content below the workspace.
